Shoulder girdle estimation from robot and IMU data

An exoskeleton holds the arm with two cuffs (green rings). From its joint angles it knows where the cuffs are, but it cannot see the shoulder girdle (clavicle and scapula). We reconstruct the girdle by fitting a shoulder model so that its arm sits in the cuffs:

  • Cuffs only: the thorax is unknown, and scapula rotations about an axis near the GH centre barely move the cuffs, so the model has to guess.
  • + thorax IMU: the thorax orientation is measured, so trunk motion is no longer mistaken for girdle motion.
  • + thorax and scapula IMU: the scapula IMU gives the direction of gravity in the scapula, which fixes its rotation.

The coloured bones are the reconstructed girdle, the white bones the true one. Simulated data from Seth et al., ideal sensors, generic exoskeleton (not the one in the paper).
